# CTSEG — Organic acquisition execution brief (16 October 2026)

## Commercial objective
Lead with **cross-sector strategic sourcing, supplier verification, export market entry and outsourced foreign-trade operations**. Glass is an existing specialist vertical, **not the homepage/SEO priority**. Preserve existing glass URLs and their rankings.

## Existing URLs to strengthen first
- /en/ — general international trade and sourcing; link prominently to sourcing, market entry, and external trade desk.
- /en/services/supplier-sourcing-and-verification/ — target *supplier sourcing Turkey*, *supplier verification Turkey* and *find manufacturers in Turkey*; add real deliverables, verification criteria, limitations and RFQ CTA.
- /en/services/market-entry/ — target *Turkey export market entry* and *find distributors in Turkey*; distinguish buyer research from guarantees.
- /tr/hizmetler/tedarikci-bulma-ve-dogrulama/ — target *Türkiye tedarikçi bulma* and *üretici doğrulama*.
- Existing trade corridors and portfolio pages — internal links to sourcing/market-entry conversion paths; avoid near-duplicate country landing pages.

## Content improvements to implement
1. Add a distinct buyer-facing FAQ answering what is checked (legal identity, manufacturing capacity, documents, sample and quotation), what evidence is delivered and what is **not guaranteed**.
2. Publish one **documented** RFQ workflow example (anonymized only with permission); never fabricate projects, client logos or order volume.
3. Make request form capture product, specifications, volume, destination and required documents; preserve current working form and privacy flow.
4. Ensure relevant links from homepage, markets, portfolio and insights to both core commercial services. Maintain language-specific canonical and hreflang.
5. Validate the site's legal-entity and founding-year claims against actual records before amplifying them; do not turn unverified claims into SEO trust signals.

## Search Console measurement
Weekly 28-day comparison: impressions, clicks, CTR and average position by query, page and country. Prioritize queries with impressions and positions 4–20. Segment branded vs nonbranded and lead-generating vs informational queries. Do not infer volume or ranking from manual spot checks.

## Acceptance criteria
Existing glass pages remain reachable and canonical; no duplicate country pages; sitemap/robots/indexability checked; primary CTAs lead to the existing RFQ workflow; build and SEO checks pass before merging/deploying.
