## Anti-spam and identity rules

- Verify CTSEG's **exact registered legal entity/name, tax and address disclosure authority, operating role** before creating any directory identity. The website's corporate claim alone is not proof.
- Represent CTSEG as *B2B sourcing and trade coordination service*, never as the manufacturer of every product in its sector portfolio.
- No fabricated office sites, production capacity, export volumes, employee count, testimonials, certifications, customer list or stock.
- Never create multiple keyword-stuffed profiles, buy SEO link packs or trade excessive reciprocal links. Google explicitly identifies low-quality directories as link spam: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies
- No paid subscriptions by default. Stop before any fee; verify exact $0 tier and automatic renewal.
- Log actual acceptance and live URL, whether publicly crawlable and whether a link is followed/nofollow **only after** the profile exists.
