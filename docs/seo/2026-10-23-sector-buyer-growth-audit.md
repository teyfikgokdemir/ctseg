## 3. Competitor / SERP-reference audit (published on-site messages, not a ranking table)

- **Generic sourcing agency:** Teminor, https://teminor.com/en — clear producer shortlist + standardized RFQ + buyer approvals. CTSEG angle: documented verification boundaries and multi-sector RFQ selection. 
- **Generic multi-category sourcing:** Supply Turk, https://www.supplyturk.com/ — clear industrial vs food entry points. CTSEG angle: eight-sector portfolio and cross-border commercial due diligence.
- **Glass:** Glass Exporter, https://www.glassexporter.com/ — glass-type categories and claimed operational capability. CTSEG must remain clearly *sourcing/coordination*, not producer.
- **Natural oils:** Roseville Wholesale, https://roseville.com.tr/WHOLESALE/ — documentary traceability and sampling; and Arovela, https://arovela.com/en/products/pure-essential-oils/ — GC-MS/COA and product-specific MOQ claims. CTSEG should state RFQ criteria rather than borrow another firm's MOQ.
- **Nuts/dried fruit:** Niyaz Food, https://niyazgida.com/en/ — grade, processing, sizes, bulk packing and explicit buyer FAQ; Nilasya Agro Foods, https://www.nilasyaagrofoods.com.tr/en/products/nuts-and-dried-fruits/ — country of origin and product specification categories.
- **Feed:** CTSEG's existing English page https://ctseg.com.tr/en/turkiye-poultry-feed-sourcing/ already separates stage-specific feed requirements; find independent comparable suppliers in sector SERPs before adding comparative claims.
- **Textiles:** DAD Textile, https://www.dadtextile.com/ — private-label development, sampling and export; Consortium Trades, https://www.consortiumtrades.com/about — hospitality procurement and single accountable point.
- **Industrial equipment:** Dantechs https://spareparts.dantechs.net/company — part-code searching, obsolete parts and verification.
- **Packaging:** Alterna, https://www.alternaas.com/ — flexible packaging supply by category; GöçTürk, https://en.gocturk.net/home — bottles/jars/pumps/airless variants.

### Differentiation to repeat consistently
**CTSEG = buyer-side trade coordination and specification-led producer research.**
Never position CTSEG as the manufacturer, stockholder or verified exporter for a product unless supported by evidence. No invented MOQ, turnaround, certificates, customer logos or buyer success figures.
