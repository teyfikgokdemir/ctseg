## On-site content checklist — use existing seven product routes
For every product: Botanical name and INCI, product differentiation (essential oil vs absolute vs hydrosol), distillation/extraction method, use case, identity/quality test requested, suitable packaging *options subject to confirmation*, storage and lot traceability, country of origin to verify, RFQ quantity + destination + Incoterms, request for sample/COA/GC-MS/SDS, relevant compliance review. No counterfeit certification badges, false stock/capacity or guaranteed quality claims. Check claims against actual supplier data before presenting as supplied.
